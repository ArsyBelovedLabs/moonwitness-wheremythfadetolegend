07 · METHODOLOGY

Read the report without jumping to conclusions.

Evidence Score measures documentation quality. Tauhid Gap is a comparative theological lens applied to a specific practice. Causality measures cause-and-effect evidence. Temporal coincidence is never treated as proof.

01Evidence first

Identify date, location, actors, primary sources, photos or video.

02Religious context

Check the community's own explanation before classifying a ritual.

03Tauhid lens

Ask who is treated as the source of power, protection, prayer or offering.

04Causality

Require a plausible mechanism before linking a ritual to a natural event.
