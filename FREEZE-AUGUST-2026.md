## Research integrity rules

Monitoring signals are discovery leads. They do not automatically become theological verdicts, actor accusations, Tauhid scores, or causal claims.

A source can be used as evidence for a documented event without proving a theological conclusion. Timing or co-occurrence does not establish causality.

The dashboard scores specific practices and evidence dimensions, not religions or populations.

## Post-freeze policy

`main` remains the live development/monitoring branch. New signals and future months must not rewrite the frozen August baseline.

Corrections to August should be represented as an explicit versioned amendment with an audit note.

September 2026 and later observations belong to new monthly datasets.
